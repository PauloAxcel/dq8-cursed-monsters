# Changelog

## v1.0 (2026-10)

First public release, PAL (SLES-53974) and USA (SLUS-21207).

- The monster team joins every battle at the start and stays until the end.
- New game with Morrie's starters; scouting, 12 slots, two teams and the Team menu from the start; all named monsters
  appear from the start.
- 150-monster bank (with paging on the release list).
- Battle menu with the team out: Fight / Flee / Away / Tactics.
- Tactics: Auto / Manual per monster; Death move Random / On / Off. Manual monsters pick move and target.
- Defeated bosses join the bank; merged forms join when you own their trio; Prince Charmles joins with the Great
  Argon Lizard. Bosses change form with their own moves.
- Team 1's first monster walks in place of the hero on the field.
- World map portraits of the recruitable monsters you don't own yet.
- Morrie revives knocked-out monsters anywhere in the bank.
- One xdelta patch per version (PAL, USA) for your own ISO.
